# About

Jezper Lorné. Born 1980. Lives outside Gothenburg with my wife and two young kids.

I'm currently Head of Product at [Mecenat](https://mecenat.com), Sweden's student loyalty platform. My background is in UX design; the path from designer to product leader has run through a decade of small companies, one founded, one podcast (*[En Podd Om Teknik](#)*), and a fair amount of change management.

Earlier, in another life, I produced trance and progressive music as **Airbase**, with a handful of other aliases. The full back-catalogue lives at [airbasemusic.com](https://airbasemusic.com); a chronological summary lives [on the music page here](/music/).

Outside of work I read a lot, cook a lot, and ride an adventure motorcycle when the season allows. My motto, for what it is worth, is *knowledge is never wasted*. I have not yet figured out what I should be when I grow up.

## What this site is

A personal home, not a portfolio. A small, slow, well-kept place to publish writing and short notes. Conversation happens elsewhere — primarily on Mastodon. The canonical version of anything I publish lives here.
